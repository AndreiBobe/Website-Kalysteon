Why Stratum
The Workflow, Reimagined

Every terrain artist knows the dance. Export, copy, open, configure, fix, re-export. Stratum removes the dance.

Without Stratum
The manual loop
✕Gaea: export each variant by hand
✕Files: move & rename manually
✕Houdini: open, HDA, parms, hython batch
✕Engine: Nanite, Lumen, LODs per asset
✕Errors: one miss, start the whole run over
Time: 4–6h per terrain set
With Stratum
One click. Done.
✓Gaea: up to 10 variants in one batch
✓Files: output auto-organized for you
✓Houdini: HDA driven automatically
✓Engine: config applied per stage
✓Errors: pre-flight catches them before the run
Time: ~5 min of setup, then your coffee break

Support
Frequently Asked Questions
What software do I need to run Stratum? ▾
Stratum is a pipeline tool for Gaea, Houdini, Unreal Engine and Unity workflows. It is not a standalone application. You need the relevant software for your stage of the pipeline.
Which versions of Houdini, Unreal, and Unity are supported? ▾
Stratum supports current and widely-used versions. Compatibility issues are extremely rare. If you encounter one, contact us.
Is commercial use allowed? ▾
The Free tier is for personal and non-commercial use. Indie and Studio licenses include commercial use rights. If your annual revenue exceeds $100,000, the commercial license option is required.
What if I find a bug or something doesn't work? ▾
Contact us at contact@kalysteon.com with a description of the issue and your system setup. We aim to respond within 48 hours.
