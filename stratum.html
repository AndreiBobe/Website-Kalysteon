What is it

One tool between your terrain and your game.

Procedural tools make a terrain in minutes. Everything after still eats your days: renaming, sorting folders, exporting, re-importing, building materials, asset by asset. Stratum automates that whole second half, from your Gaea graph to assets ready in your engine.

Made for

Artists and studios who produce terrains in batches: packs, variants, whole biomes.

It removes

The repetitive work between tools, done by hand for every single asset.

You get

Hours back on every set, and assets that land named, sorted and ready to use.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

What do I need installed to use Stratum?

Gaea 2 Professional or Enterprise, plus Unreal Engine or Unity for the import stage. Houdini is optional. Stratum drives those tools, it does not replace them.

Does it work with Gaea Community?

No. Stratum builds your graphs through Gaea's command line, which is reserved for Gaea 2 Professional and Enterprise. You can still use the Houdini and engine stages on meshes you already have.

Can I use it without Houdini?

Yes. Every stage can be switched off. Gaea straight to Unreal, Gaea straight to Unity, or Gaea on its own are all valid runs.

How do updates work?

Stratum tells you when a new version is out. Download the installer and run it over the current one: your projects, settings and license stay in place.

Can I sell games made with Stratum terrains?

With an Indie or Studio license, yes. The free tier is personal and non-commercial. Terrains you generate belong to you in every tier.
